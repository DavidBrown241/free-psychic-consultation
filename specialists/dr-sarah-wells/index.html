About Sarah

How Sarah works.

Dr. Sarah Wells founded Free Psychic Consult and still takes consultations herself. She is described on the platform as a licensed counsellor and intuitive advisor, and her sessions reflect both halves of that description. She brings the structure of a counselling conversation, with careful questions, reflection and attention to pacing, together with the intuitive impressions that led her to build a service around a free first consultation.

Her specialty is relationship crossroads and major life transitions. That might mean a separation, a reconciliation attempt, a marriage that has changed shape, a move across the country, a child leaving home, retirement, or the odd emptiness that follows a long-awaited goal. People who work with Sarah often say they were looking for someone who would stay level while they talked through a messy situation. She is known for a calm, professional demeanour and for specific observations that connect what you said at the start with what you said at the end.

Sarah is careful about what she will and will not do. She does not diagnose, does not treat mental-health conditions and does not give legal or financial advice. Intuitive guidance on this platform is offered for reflection, and she will say so openly. If a conversation shows signs of crisis, abuse or risk to someone's safety, she pauses the session and encourages the person to contact emergency services, a crisis line or a qualified professional in their area.

A consultation with Sarah usually has a clear arc. She begins by asking what has changed and what you are hoping will be different afterward. She then offers a few impressions, tests them against your own account, and helps you sort the situation into what is within your control and what is not. You leave with a short list of next steps and a few questions to consider. Your first five minutes are free, so you can judge whether her approach is right for you before deciding to continue.

The session

How a consultation with Sarah goes.

  1. Say what is on your mind

    Start with a sentence or two. Sarah will ask a few grounding questions before offering any impressions.

  2. Hear impressions, test them

    Impressions are offered as prompts. You decide what fits and what does not, and you can pause or redirect at any time.

  3. Decide what happens next

    At five minutes you choose: end the session at no charge, or continue at the posted rate with a visible running total.

Questions to bring

Questions Sarah likes to work with.

  • Should I stay or leave this relationship?
  • How do I begin again after a big change?
  • What is within my control in this situation?
  • How do I talk to my family about my decision?
  • What do I need before I can move forward?
