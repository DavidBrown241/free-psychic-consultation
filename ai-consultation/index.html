    FAQ

    Good to know.

    Is this a live session with the consultant?

    No. The AI Consultation is an automated tool. It writes a short reflection in the style of the consultant you choose. For a real conversation, start your free first five minutes with a live consultant.

    Do you keep what I write?

    Your text is sent only to produce your brief and is not added to any account or profile. Please avoid names, addresses and other details that identify you or other people.

    Can the brief tell me what will happen?

    No. It offers impressions, patterns and questions to consider. It does not predict outcomes or what another person thinks, and it is not medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice.

    What if I am in a crisis?

    This tool is not the right place. If you or someone else is in danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis line right away, or reach out to a doctor or someone you trust.
